Vacation rentals and home swaps, by the numbers

Short-term rental bookings, occupancy and rates, plus home-exchange membership and exchange volumes, from published market reports.

Two different travel markets sit behind this name. One rents a villa by the night; the other swaps a home for a home, with no money changing hands for the stay itself. Both are growing, but they are measured differently, and the headline totals on this page must not be mixed: Phocuswright counts gross bookings (the value of stays booked), while Grand View counts market revenue under its own definition. They describe the same broad market from different angles, and this page keeps them in separate, labeled rows.

What the 2025 numbers show is a market that has stopped expanding by adding listings and started growing by filling the ones it has: U.S. supply growth fell to its weakest rate on record in July 2025, while full-year occupancy still ticked up and Airbnb's booked nights grew 8%. On the swap side, the two leading communities both reported their fastest membership growth in 2024–2025.

How to read these numbers

Included: Phocuswright global and North America gross-booking figures and U.S. usage incidence (July 2026 report summary); Grand View Research U.S. revenue and segment figures (March 2026); AirDNA measured U.S. occupancy, rate, supply, and December 2025 snapshot data (January 2026); Airbnb full-year 2025 company results; U.S. Travel Association 2026 forecasts as reported by Business Travel News; and company-reported HomeExchange and Kindred membership and exchange volumes. Excluded: Statista figures visible only in secondary compilations, undated homepage claims presented as dated figures, and any market total that blends revenue and gross-booking definitions.

Forecasts are labeled as forecasts wherever they appear. Platform membership and exchange figures are company-reported, not independently audited, and are shown with their reporting dates so a reader can judge their age.
